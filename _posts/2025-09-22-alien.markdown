Alien: Earth is a good sci-fi show! Very good - one of the best in many years.

But is it a good Alien show..? I don't think so, personally. The basic rules of an Alien movie are:

1. The Alien is fucking terrifying.
2. No really, the Alien is so terrifying that any attempts to harness and control its power fail horribly.

(yes, I know it's called a xenomorph but Alien is shorter)

These rules are why Alien films work so well: First we get scared on a visceral level when we see the Alien toy with characters we care about before slaughtering them. It's unstoppable. Second, we learn that Evil Management is trying to use the Alien somehow, but it is just so wildly powerful that containment always fails; the Alien is terrifying at that level as well.

Even the Alien vs Predator movies get this right, when the second part is that the Predators tried to use Aliens for hunting sport (and look how it turned out for them). TODO watch end of movie 2

Alien: Earth goes a different way.

First, while we do see the Alien slaughter people, we have to wait many episodes before it kills a named character. And we see the Alien in, um, embarrassing situations: grabbed awkwardly by the tongue, later struggling as it is wrestled with. That's like showing the Alien doing its taxes: it's not scary.

That there are other monsters in the show - some arguably worse! - also works against the Alien being especially fearsome.

And the second rule is also broken [as of episode 7]: The Alien is communicated with, and in fact turned into a helpful puppy, doing exactly what Wendy wants it to do. It turns out it *can* be harnessed and controlled.

As I said, I like Alien: Earth as a sci-show. It feels like a sort of extended Black Mirror episode, really, with the "soul transfer" of the hybrids that the plot revolves around. But the Alien is not The Alien.

"Fixing" Alien: Earth

What is the smallest change to Alien: Earth that would fix those problems and make it feel more like an Alien movie?

* Put the spaceship episode first. Ideally expand it to two episodes: let us get to know some characters, then see them die. Note: When I saw this episode the first time I thought "ah, they delayed it to now to prepare us for blood-bug epidemic in the next episode", but... that didn't happen. I can't actually figure out what reason they had for putting it there. The episode ends with M returning home to Weyland-Yutani, but not as a result of anything we learned in the episode... so it can just be first?

* No tongue-twisting dance with Wendy. If you need to get her and the Alien away from her brothers' eyes, have him barely survive an attack and lose consciousness briefly. If you want to show the Alien avoiding harm to Wendy, but all means do that, but in a spooky/creepy way. That "what are you doing?" her brother asks and she responds "I don't know" could be fun comedy relief in general, and sure, humor can have a place in an Alien movie, *but not in a scene with the Alien.*

* No piggyback-wrestling scene. If you want to show the eye-monster is scary, show it being intelligent, or show it taking over that human body in the first place. Don't show a physical body - the prey species for the Alien - riding it while the Alien flails about, seemingly helpless.

* Wendy dies to the Alien. She harnessed and controlled it, so by the rules, that has to fail horribly.
