*(spoilers for Alien: Earth season 1)*

When is a xenomorph *not* a xenomorph?

When you show it behaving like a loyal dog. Tame, docile, controlled.

The basic rules of an Alien movie are:

1. **The Alien is absolutely terrifying.**
2. **No really, the Alien is so terrifying that any attempts to harness and control its power fail horribly.**

From **Alien (1979)** to **Alien: Romulus (2024)** - even including the oddball **Alien vs Predator** movies - this recipe works: First, we get scared on a visceral level when we see the Alien slaughter characters we care about. Second, we learn that Evil Management is trying to use the Alien somehow, but it is just so wildly powerful that containment always fails.

But **Alien: Earth (2025)** goes a different way:

* Many episodes pass before the Alien kills a named character.
* We see the Alien in, um, embarrassing situations: grabbed awkwardly by the tongue, later struggling as it is wrestled with.
* The Alien becomes Wendy's loyal servant: It *can* be harnessed and controlled. Easily, in fact!

This is not a show about a terrifying Alien and the ghastly consequences of meddling with it. This is a show about the hybrid children, superbeings whose souls were transported from their dying bodies à la Black Mirror, in conflict with their "parents". And with some dangerous alien lifeforms in the mix (in fact, most of them more horrifying than the Alien).

The show has many good parts: Timothy Olyphant, the eye-monster, some perfectly-delivered lines like the one I paraphrase in the title. But if **Alien: Earth** had an actual xenomorph, it would rip Wendy into shreds.
